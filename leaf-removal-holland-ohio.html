Questions homeowners ask

Quick answers before you request an estimate.

Do you do leaf removal for Holland businesses?Yes. Commercial frontage along Airport Highway and McCord Road can be scoped alongside residential cleanups, and the leaves are hauled away with the crew.
Why clear leaves out of the wet spots?Leaves that settle in wet lawn pockets hold water, mat down, and smother the grass under them. Clearing them before the ground freezes keeps those spots from coming back thin in spring.
What is included in a Terramorph fall cleanup?Leaves cleared from the lawn, beds, fence lines, and under shrubs; spent perennials cut back and annuals pulled; light shrub shaping; sticks, brush, and debris hauled off; bed edges cleaned up; and final mows when they are part of the plan. The leaves and debris leave with the crew.
Is it bad to leave leaves on the lawn over winter?A thick layer blocks light and can leave thin, matted patches and snow mold in spring. Ohio State University Extension recommends removing leaves once they build up past about two inches.
Do you cut back every plant in the beds?No. Spent and diseased perennials get cut back and annuals come out. Spring-flowering shrubs like lilac, forsythia, and azalea are not sheared in fall because next spring's flower buds are already set. Ornamental grasses and seed heads can stay up for winter interest if you want.

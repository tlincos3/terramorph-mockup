Questions homeowners ask

Quick answers before you request an estimate.

Still wondering about something? Call 419-873-6801, 7 a.m. to 8 p.m.

Do you offer seasonal snow removal contracts?

Yes. Terramorph offers seasonal winter service for driveways, walkways, and commercial lots in Wood and Lucas County. Late summer and fall are the best time to lock in a spot before routes fill.

Do you plow residential driveways or just commercial lots?

Both. Terramorph handles residential driveways and walkways as well as commercial parking lots and property access.

When do you come out after it snows?

Service is triggered by snowfall depth agreed on in the contract. Routes run so driveways and lots are open when people need to leave for work or open for business.

Do you handle ice and salting too?

Yes. Ice management and salting can be included so walkways, entries, and lots stay safe, not just cleared.

What areas do you cover for snow removal?

Perrysburg, Toledo, Maumee, Sylvania, Rossford, and surrounding Wood and Lucas County communities.

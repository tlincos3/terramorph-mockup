What we light

Paths, entries, patios, and the trees that frame the house.

Low-voltage lighting is planned around how you move through the property at night and what you want people to notice from the street.

  1. Path lightingWalkways and steps lit so family and guests can see where they are going.
  2. Accent lightingTrees, stone, and plantings lit to give the house depth after dark.
  3. Patio and entertaining areasLight for the outdoor spaces you actually use at night.
  4. Entry lightingFront doors and drives that look welcoming and are easy to find.
  5. Security visibilityFewer dark corners around the house, garage, and side yards.

Planned with the yard

Lighting works best when it is planned with the patio and beds.

When lighting is part of a bigger project, wire runs go in before pavers and plants, so nothing gets torn up later. It can also be added to an existing yard.

  • Fixtures placed for the view from the street and from inside
  • Wire runs planned around patios, beds, and walkways
  • Added to new projects or existing landscapes

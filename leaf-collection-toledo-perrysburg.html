The short answer

The three cities handle leaves three different ways. Toledo collects loose leaves raked to the curb, working through the city by ZIP code. Perrysburg takes leaves through its yard-waste program, in paper bags or carts that each need a city tag. Maumee collects loose leaves by district, for owner-occupied homes only.

Pickup weeks change every year and can shift during the season, so this guide links to each city's own page instead of listing dates that will go stale. Everything below was checked against the city websites on November 24, 2026.

City of Toledo leaf collection

Toledo runs a curbside leaf collection every fall. According to the City of Toledo leaf collection page, the 2026 program began Monday, November 2 and is scheduled to run about six weeks, with the schedule set by ZIP code and updates released on Fridays.

The city asks residents to rake leaves to the curb line no more than two days before their scheduled pickup, keep storm drain inlets clear, and keep cars off the street when pickup is scheduled. If you miss collection, the city says not to place leaves in the street. Its page lists composting or mulching, your trash container, or a city drop-off site as the options, with drop-off open through January 8, 2027.

Your ZIP code's week is on the city page. Check it rather than a neighbor's memory of last year, because the order changes.

Perrysburg leaf collection

Perrysburg handles leaves as yard waste. The City of Perrysburg yard waste page lists leaves among the materials collected in 30-gallon paper yard waste bags or in 64- and 96-gallon carts the resident buys, and says each bag or cart needs an official city yard waste tag for every collection. That page does not describe a loose-leaf curb pickup.

From April 1 through November 30, the city says yard waste is collected on regular garbage days. From December 1 through March 31, collection is on Wednesdays only and has to be requested ahead of time by calling Modern Disposal at 419-874-2966. Tag locations and current tag fees are on the city page.

For a Perrysburg yard with one or two trees, that is a manageable number of bags. For a wooded lot, it is a lot of bagging and tagging, and the regular weekly route ends right around the time the last leaves come down.

Maumee leaf collection

Maumee collects loose leaves by district. The City of Maumee fall leaf collection page says collection typically begins in October, the city is divided into four districts, and a schedule with pickup dates is posted each year. Leaves go behind the curb in front of your own property by the Sunday before your district's week, with no sticks, grass, or other debris mixed in, and not in the street or on storm drains.

The service is for owner-occupied homes with two or fewer units. The city says rental properties, condominiums, apartment buildings, commercial properties, and mobile home parks are not included. It also says landscape contractors and anyone removing yard waste for hire may not place leaves at the curb for city collection.

That last rule matters if you hire help. A crew working in Maumee has to haul the leaves away, which is how Terramorph works everywhere.

Sylvania, Rossford, Oregon, Bowling Green, and other towns

Every city, village, and township in Wood and Lucas County sets its own leaf rules, and some change them from year to year. If you live outside the three cities above, check your city's leaf collection page or call the public service department before you rake anything to the street.

When city leaf pickup is all you need

City pickup is a good deal when the yard is small or lightly treed, you have the time and the back to rake, your trees drop before your scheduled week, and your property qualifies for the program. Plenty of homeowners in all three cities never need more than that.

If that is your yard, use the city program. A light layer of leaves can also be mulched into the lawn with a mower instead of being moved at all. Once leaves build up past about two inches, Ohio State University Extension recommends removing them so the grass underneath is not smothered.

When hiring a leaf removal crew makes more sense

The city truck takes what is at the curb on its week. It does not get leaves out of the beds, off the fence line, from under the shrubs, or out of the backyard. These are the situations where people call us instead.

The lot is heavily wooded. A half acre of mature oak and maple is days of raking, and wet leaves are heavy.

The trees drop late. Oaks hold leaves into late November and December, after many pickup weeks have passed.

You missed your week, or you travel, or the weather did not cooperate.

The property does not qualify. Rentals, condos, and commercial properties in Maumee are not covered by city collection.

Bagging is the bottleneck. In Perrysburg, a big yard means a long row of tagged bags.

You want the beds done too. Perennials cut back, bed edges cleaned up, and the lawn ready for winter in the same visit.
